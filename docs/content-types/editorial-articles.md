## What it is

Written interviews and articles connected to artists and / or Series. On an artist profile this appears as an "In Their Words" section with a pull quote and a link to the full interview. The full article has its own page and publishing state.

## Record

- Title, slug, body (rich text), images, pull quote, summary.
- Publish status, scheduled / published dates.

## Relationships

- Artists (many-to-many).
- Series (many-to-many).

## V1 behaviours

- Create and edit written content with images.
- Connect to artists and / or Series.
- Draft, schedule, publish.
- Full article page linked from the profile / Series experience.

## Build options

| Option | Implication |
|---|---|
| Use the platform CMS | Keeps administration in one place |
| Separate editorial platform | Adds another system and an integration |
