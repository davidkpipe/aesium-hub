## What it is

Behind the Scenes, Live Sessions, Music Videos and Event Recordings, shown within Artist Profile Experiences and connected to Series and Events in V1. Audience members can like and save videos.

## Record

- Title, slug, description, thumbnail / artwork.
- Video Category: Behind the Scenes / Live Sessions / Music Videos / Event Recordings.
- Video reference (provider id / embed), duration.
- Publish status, scheduled / published dates.

## Relationships

- Artists (many-to-many).
- Series (optional).
- Event (for Event Recordings).
- Likes and saves; view events.

## V1 behaviours

- Upload / manage records; publish, schedule, unpublish.
- Display in Artist Profile Experiences and other confirmed placements.
- Receive viewing analytics from the provider.

## Open questions

- Required quality, upload limits and viewing volume.
- Audiovisual rights and attendee permissions for filmed events. Rights are separate from DJ mix audio licensing.
